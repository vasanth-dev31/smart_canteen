QR CODE SETUP
==============
The QR should point to the deployed Smart Canteen home URL.

For local testing, use:
http://127.0.0.1:5000/

For a college LAN deployment, use the computer's LAN IP, for example:
http://192.168.x.x:5000/

For internet deployment, replace it with the public HTTPS URL.

The /qr route redirects to the home page and can also be used as the QR destination.
